\section{Introduction}
Current generative models for trajectory prediction, such as the Conditional Flow Matching model (MoFlow) \cite{fu2025moflow}, rely strictly on historical kinematic data to predict future coordinates. However, past trajectories are often insufficient to explain future movements when environmental structures or obstacles constrain an agent's path.

In this work, we propose Video-MoFlow. We formulate trajectory forecasting as a video-conditioned flow matching problem, where the future motion distribution is generated by a flow field modulated by both past kinematics and visual evidence. We specifically introduce a global video encoder to process environmental context to investigate its influence on the predicted probability paths. This report outlines the current architecture and provides an empirical evaluation of its preliminary performance.
