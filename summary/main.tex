\begin{abstract}
Human trajectory forecasting requires modeling complex, multi-modal future movements. We extend the one-step flow matching model, MoFlow, by integrating rich visual evidence from video inputs. By conditioning the generative flow field on global visual tokens, our proposed Video-MoFlow significantly reduces spatial ambiguity. Preliminary results on the ETH/UCY dataset demonstrate the efficacy of our global video encoder in improving trajectory precision.
\end{abstract}
